# Amazon Product Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Amazon Product Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze product pricing, discounts, ratings, customer engagement, and category performance.

The dashboard transforms raw Amazon product data into actionable business insights that help stakeholders understand customer behavior, evaluate pricing strategies, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive view of:

- Product performance across categories
- Customer satisfaction through ratings and reviews
- Discount effectiveness
- Popularity vs product quality analysis
- Revenue contribution by category

This dashboard enables business stakeholders to quickly identify trends, opportunities, and areas requiring improvement.

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### 1. Average Discount by Category

Displays products/categories receiving the highest average discounts.

**Business Insight:**
- Helps identify aggressive discounting strategies.
- Useful for evaluating promotional campaigns.

---

### 2. Popularity vs Quality Analysis

Scatter Plot comparing:

- Product Rating
- Rating Count

**Business Insight:**
- Identifies products with both high popularity and high customer satisfaction.
- Highlights products receiving large engagement despite lower ratings.

---

### 3. Price Filter (Slicer)

Interactive price range filter allowing users to analyze products across different pricing segments.

**Business Value:**
- Enables targeted product analysis.
- Supports pricing strategy evaluation.

---

### 4. Category-wise Product Count

Shows product distribution across categories.

**Business Insight:**
- Identifies dominant product categories.
- Helps understand inventory concentration.

---

### 5. Product Performance Table

Displays detailed product-level metrics:

- Product ID
- Actual Price
- Rating
- Rating Count

**Business Value:**
- Supports detailed product comparison.
- Enables drill-down analysis.

---

### 6. Discounted Price by Review Content

Analyzes discounted price distribution against review patterns.

**Business Insight:**
- Helps understand customer perception of discounted products.
- Identifies products generating positive engagement.

---

### 7. Revenue Distribution by Category

Donut chart showing category contribution based on actual prices.

**Business Insight:**
- Highlights major revenue-generating categories.
- Helps prioritize high-value segments.

## 📋 Key Insights Generated

### Pricing Analysis
- Significant discounts are concentrated within selected product categories.
- Several premium products maintain strong customer engagement despite higher prices.

### Customer Satisfaction
- Most products maintain ratings above 4.0.
- Higher-rated products generally receive larger rating counts.

### Product Popularity
- A small group of products contributes disproportionately to customer engagement.
- Popularity and quality show a positive correlation.

### Category Performance
- Electronics categories dominate both product count and revenue contribution.
- Certain categories generate high revenue despite lower product volume.
